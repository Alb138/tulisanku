TULISANKU

Draw a few letters. Get your whole handwriting as a font you can type with.

  1. Draw at least 5 characters - more letters, closer match.
  2. AI learns your style and writes every other letter and digit.
  3. Type with your handwriting in Word, Docs or any app.

Your drawings are only used to make your font. They are not stored, and the finished font is forgotten after an hour unless you download it.

Write resting on the line, as you normally would.

Learning your handwriting…

The AI reads your style from the letters you drew and writes the rest.

AI marks letters the AI wrote; the others are exactly as you drew them. Tap any letter to draw it yourself.

Download font

To install: double-click the downloaded file and choose Install, then pick in Word or any other app. The font is released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Characters other than 0-9, A-Z, a-z and . , : ; - ' are not included yet.
